## Design choice (recorded per panel instruction)

The HIGH divergence (produce path resolved categories WITH the theme tiebreak,
feed path WITHOUT — same story, chip "sport", bucket "arts") had two candidate
fixes: thread the theme map through every feed seam, or fold everything into ONE
resolve-once verdict map. **Chosen: the resolve-once map** (Addendum-2 steer):
`compute_category_verdicts` (agents/pipeline/stages/ranking.py) computes one
`{story_id: FeedCategory}` verdict per batch in `run_daily_pipeline` and rides the
existing `category_override_by_story` seam to caps, ceiling, shortlist, feed
assembly AND (as `pinned_segment_slug` through `write_phase`) the persisted
`story_segment_slug`. Rationale: one verdict per story instead of 5+ sites to keep
in sync; subsumes the persist-divergence finding; lets the reconcile merge-pin
machinery dissolve (provably identical resolver). It also fixes the conflict-log
double-fire: downstream `assign_category` calls short-circuit on the override
before any logging, so `category_conflict_lowest_depth_won` fires once per story.
